Black letters.One orange ray

XRAY is one name with one spark of color. The letters carry no color of their own, so they sit beside any product name as one phrase. The ray is the slash in XRAY/Design, drawn.

  • WordmarkBlack on light, white on dark

01

Logo

Three parts: the drawn wordmark, the ray, and a product name set in the interface font. They come in one line or in two. The wordmark takes the text color of its surface. Only the ray is orange.

A

One line

The logo wherever there is width for it: headers, footers, signatures, anything set in a row.

  1. WordmarkBlack on light, white on dark. Never orange, never gray, never a gradient.
  2. RayOrange #F9601F on every surface. It rises 55% above the letters and is the only part that does. It stands between the Y's right side and the name, with no space on either side.
  3. Product nameThe interface font, weight 650, mixed case, tracking −1%. Its size is 17/23 of the logo's height. It sits on the baseline and starts where the ray ends.
  • Clear spaceThe height of the letters, on every side.
  • Smallest size16px of logo height. Below that, use the icon.
B

Two lines

The same parts where a row is too narrow or the name is long: square formats, columns, covers. The name wraps under the letters, and the ray runs on down behind it.

  1. WordmarkThe letters, unchanged, on the first line.
  2. RayIn its place after the Y, with the same tip. Its two edges run on down to the name's baseline: the same ray, 1.77 times as long.
  3. Product nameWrapped to the second line, starting on the X's left edge, and set over the ray where they cross. The same size and weight as in one line; its baseline is 1.2 letter heights under the letters'.
  • Clear spaceThe height of the letters, on every side.
  • Smallest size16px from the top of the letters to the name's baseline. Below that, use the icon.

One orange X, in two forms. In a browser tab it stands alone. Where a platform needs a filled shape, an app icon or a profile picture, it sits on black. There is no room for the ray at these sizes, so the X carries the orange.

06

Slash

Every product is XRAY, a slash, a name. Typed, it is a plain slash. Drawn, it is the ray. Repeated, it is a field: quiet slashes that a ray lights as it passes.

The field · move the pointer to draw a ray · click to launch one
Typed

XRAY/Design

In running text, file names and addresses: a plain slash in the text's own color, with no spaces around it.

Drawn

Design

In the logo and in headlines the slash is the ray: orange, on the baseline, rising above the letters.

Repeated
  1. GridA slash every 26px, in the muted text color, strongest at the top center.
  2. RayA band of slashes turned to 45°, a point at the top widening to 120–170px, in orange.
  3. TimingDraws in about a second, holds for two, then dissolves into small symbols.
  4. PointerMoving draws a short ray through it. A click launches a full one.
07

Patterns

Six patterns from two shapes, the X and the ray. Each works in three colorways: switch them on the tile, and take the SVG. They are vector, so they scale from a favicon strip to a stage wall.

Using patterns

  • One pattern per surface, in one colorway.
  • Keep text off the dense part: every pattern has a quiet side.
  • The marks are never outlined, rotated freely or recolored outside the three colorways.

09

Don't

Fifteen ways the identity breaks. Everything else is allowed.

  1. Don't color the letters orange.The wordmark is black or white. Only the ray is orange.
  2. Don't recolor the ray.It is orange, or the logo's one color when there is only one.
  3. Don't stretch, slant or rotate the logo.Scale it whole, in proportion.
  4. Don't add effects.No gradients, shadows, outlines or glows.
  5. Don't crowd the logo.Keep the height of the letters clear on every side.
  6. Don't go below 16px of logo height.Smaller than that, use the X.
  7. Don't set the product name in capitals or bold.It is a second voice: mixed case, semibold, on the baseline.
  8. Don't use the ray alone as a logo.In text and in patterns it is an ornament. The icon is the X.
  9. Don't make the X white, or any other color.Alone or on black, the X is orange.
  10. Don't put the favicon on a tile.In a browser tab the X stands alone. The black square is for app icons and avatars.
  11. Don't set links in #F9601F on light.It is 3.1:1 there. Links take #D14300, and #FF8A52 on dark.
  12. Don't change the button.Orange with a white label; the outline button for everything secondary.
  13. Don't put more than two rays in a line.And never one inside a word.
  14. Don't mix colorways.One pattern per surface, in orange, black or paper.
  15. Don't write Xray or X-Ray.The name is XRAY, in capitals. Products follow a slash: XRAY/Design.
