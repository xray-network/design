# Theming

Pick a primary color, the tone of the grays and a font. The kit's colors are derived from them for light and dark
theme: the accent shades for fills, hover and press; a lighter or darker ink where the color is drawn thin on the
canvas, so links, focus rings and icons stay readable; and grays tinted warm or cold. The example shows the result
on the kit, and the prompt under it carries every value to your coding agent, which writes them into your
[theme layer](/concepts#tokens-and-themes) so upgrades keep your brand.

Your theme stays in this browser and is applied to every preview in this wiki, so you can see any component in it.
The showcase apps keep the kit's look. **Reset to the kit** removes it.

## What changes

| Setting | What it changes |
|---|---|
| Primary color | Buttons, selections, switches, links, focus rings and the first chart series. Fills keep your color; where it is drawn thin on the canvas it is darkened in light theme and lightened in dark theme until it reads (4.5:1 and 6:1). Text on a fill is white, or near black when white would not read. |
| Tone | The grays of backgrounds, borders and text, in both themes: warm, cold, or the kit's own (default). Each step keeps the kit's lightness, so contrast stays the same. |
| Font | Any Google font; the prompt has your agent load it and keep the system fonts as fallbacks. |

Everything else stays as the kit has it: status colors, spacing, corners, motion and how components work. The full
rule is in the [design protocol](/protocol), section 11.
