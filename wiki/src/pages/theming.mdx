# Theming

Theming changes the look of every component at once: your brand color, fonts, corner radius and grays, in any GUI
framework. Your agent writes your values into your [theme layer](/concepts#tokens-and-themes), so upgrades keep
your brand.

## Generate a theme prompt

Enter your brand (turn on **Show preview** to see it on kit components), pick your framework, then send the prompt
to your coding agent. It derives
the full set of colors for light and dark theme, writes them to your theme layer and checks contrast.

## What you can change

| Part | How it changes |
|---|---|
| Brand color | Buttons, selections, links, focus rings and icons, derived from one color. Fills keep your color; links, focus rings and icons get a lighter shade in dark theme, so they stay readable. |
| Font | Any font your project already loads, with the system fonts as fallbacks. |
| Corners | Sharper or rounder, scaled together so nested corners still fit. Round controls stay round. |
| Grays | Backgrounds, borders and text, cooler, warmer or pure neutral, in both themes. |

Only the brand color is required; font, corners and grays keep the kit's look unless you change them. Everything
else stays as it is: both themes, readable contrast, status colors, spacing, motion and how components
work. The full rule is in the [design protocol](/protocol), section 11.
