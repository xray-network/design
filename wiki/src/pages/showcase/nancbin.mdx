# Nancbin

<AppShowcase id="nancbin" />

An exchange in three screens. Markets is a Segmented filter over List rows with prices and changes; Trade is a price with its period switch and the Swap block; the wallet is the Balance block over the asset list.

Every screen is a static HTML page on the kit's own CSS and controllers. The bottom tabs are a LiquidIndicator
and link the screens to each other, so each frame is a working app. **Open** shows a screen on its own; **Copy
prompt** gives your agent that page to rebuild in your project.
