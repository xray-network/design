# Showcase

Fifteen whole apps built from kit components, blocks, charts and motion, with no styles of their own beyond
layout. Each app is one plain HTML page: its screens are sections, and the tab bar moves between them with the
kit's Screen pattern, without loading a page. Open any screen on its own, or copy its prompt and your agent builds the same
screen in your project.

## Mobile first

Familiar phone apps, in a column a phone wide: social, messaging, money, music, travel and mail.
