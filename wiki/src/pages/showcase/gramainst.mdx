# Gramainst

<AppShowcase id="gramainst" />

A photo feed in three screens. The feed stacks Post blocks under a row of stories; Explore is a search field, a row of filter chips and a grid; the profile is the Profile block over the same grid. The pictures are abstract art from the Avatar's generator, because the kit ships no photography.

Every screen is a static HTML page on the kit's own CSS and controllers. The bottom tabs are a LiquidIndicator
and link the screens to each other, so each frame is a working app. **Open** shows a screen on its own; **Copy
prompt** gives your agent that page to rebuild in your project.
