# Celver App

<AppShowcase id="celver" />

A hosting console in three screens. Status is a List of services with Sparklines, a StatusStrip of the last 60 days and a ChartCard of response time; Deployments is a Timeline; Usage is a DonutChart beside the UsageMeters block, which warns as a limit gets near.

The app is one static HTML page on the kit's own CSS and controllers: every screen is a section, and the tabs in
the TopBar block move between them with the Screen pattern. It is designed 740px wide and is framed here at 640px.
**Open** shows a screen on its own; **Copy prompt** gives your agent that page to rebuild in your project.
