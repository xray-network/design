# Mailg App

<AppShowcase id="mailg" />

A mail app in five screens. The inbox is the Inbox block under a Segmented filter, unread messages marked; a message is its text, its attachments as List rows, and the Composer block docked at the foot to reply; a new message is three fields; Sent is the Inbox block again; settings are MenuList groups.

The app is one static HTML page on the kit's own CSS and controllers: every screen is a section, and the bottom
tabs are the TabBar block, a LiquidIndicator that moves between them with the Screen pattern, so nothing reloads.
**Open** shows a screen on its own; **Copy prompt** gives your agent that page to rebuild in your project.
