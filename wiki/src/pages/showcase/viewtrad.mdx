# Viewtrad App

<AppShowcase id="viewtrad" />

A markets terminal in three screens. Markets is a ChartCard of the portfolio over the MarketList block and a DonutChart; a market is a ChartCard with a CandlestickChart, then the OrderBook block beside a DepthChart of the same orders; Map is a Treemap of the largest S&P 500 companies, sized by market cap and colored by the day's move. The figures are samples, not live quotes.

The app is one static HTML page on the kit's own CSS and controllers: every screen is a section, and the tabs in
the TopBar block move between them with the Screen pattern. It is designed 740px wide and is framed here at 640px.
**Open** shows a screen on its own; **Copy prompt** gives your agent that page to rebuild in your project.
