# Pestri App

<AppShowcase id="pestri" />

A payments dashboard in four screens. Home is Stats, a ChartCard with a LineChart of volume, and a List of the latest payments; Payments is the TableView block: search, a Segmented filter, the Table and its pages; a row opens the RecordDetail block, with the payment's facts and a Timeline; Settings is the Settings block, a full form in sections.

The app is one static HTML page on the kit's own CSS and controllers: every screen is a section, and the tabs in
the TopBar block move between them with the Screen pattern. It is designed 740px wide and is framed here at 640px.
**Open** shows a screen on its own; **Copy prompt** gives your agent that page to rebuild in your project.
