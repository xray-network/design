# Bnbair App

<AppShowcase id="bnbair" />

A stays app in five screens. Explore is a search field, Chips and places to stay; a listing opens with a Pager of photos you can swipe, then its facts as List rows; booking is the Calendar picking a range of nights over the Checkout block; Trips is a List with an Empty state; the profile is the Profile block and a MenuList.

The app is one static HTML page on the kit's own CSS and controllers: every screen is a section, and the bottom
tabs are the TabBar block, a LiquidIndicator that moves between them with the Screen pattern, so nothing reloads.
**Open** shows a screen on its own; **Copy prompt** gives your agent that page to rebuild in your project.
