# Tifyspo App

<AppShowcase id="tifyspo" />

A music app in four screens. Home is a row of covers and a List of tracks, with the Player block as a mini bar docked above the tab bar; pressing it opens the full Player, with its Slider to scrub; Search is Chips over a MediaGrid; the library is a Segmented switch over a List of playlists.

The app is one static HTML page on the kit's own CSS and controllers: every screen is a section, and the bottom
tabs are the TabBar block, a LiquidIndicator that moves between them with the Screen pattern, so nothing reloads.
**Open** shows a screen on its own; **Copy prompt** gives your agent that page to rebuild in your project.
