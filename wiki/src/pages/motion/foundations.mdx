# Foundations

Every pattern in the Motion section is made of the same few parts. They are tokens and small helpers, so a
movement the kit does not have can be built to the same rules.

## Durations and curves

Motion reads its timing from tokens, never from numbers in a component.

| Token | Value | For |
|---|---|---|
| `--x-duration-fast` | 120ms | Hover and press colors, the start of an exit |
| `--x-duration-base` | 180ms | Small reveals, fades |
| `--x-duration-slow` | 280ms | Panels, layout, exits of large surfaces |
| `--x-ease` | `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` | Color, opacity, every exit, layout that must not wobble |
| `--x-spring` with `--x-spring-duration` | a `linear()` curve, 390ms | Things that travel and may pass their target a little: indicators, thumbs, a drag let go |
| `--x-spring-soft` with `--x-spring-soft-duration` | a `linear()` curve, 517ms | Larger moves with no overshoot: reflow, morph, screens |

Three rules decide which to use: what arrives may spring, what leaves eases, and nothing takes longer than 600ms.

## Springs

A spring is a curve with a duration that belongs to it: stiffer is quicker, less damping overshoots more. The
kit writes its springs as CSS `linear()` curves, generated by `spring()` in `core/motion.js`, so they run in
plain CSS transitions with no library. To change the kit's feel, make a spring here and set the two tokens in
your theme.

## Reduced motion

When the device asks for less motion, or an ancestor has `data-motion="reduce"`, every duration token becomes 0.
A pattern built on the tokens needs nothing else: transitions end at once, and the helpers check the same
tokens before they animate. Reduced motion removes movement, never content: what would have slid in is simply
there.

## Helpers

`core/motion.js` has no dependencies. The patterns are these helpers applied.

| Helper | What it does | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| `duration(el, token)`, `easing(el, token)` | A token's value where the element sits, so reduced motion and themes are respected | every controller |
| `reduced(el)` | Whether motion is reduced for the element | Pager |
| `trackIndicator(root, getActive)` | Places one indicator under the active item and moves it, leading edge first | Liquid Indicator, Segmented |
| `createFlip(getElements)` | Measures elements, then plays them from where they were to where they are | Toast, Message |
| `afterTransition(el, fn)` | Runs after an exit transition, or at once when motion is reduced | Toast |
| `spring(options)` | Makes a `linear()` curve and its duration from stiffness and damping | the tokens above |

Each pattern adds its own controller on top: `morph()`, `reflow()`, `bindDrag()`, `bindReorder()`,
`screenTransition()`, `replace()`, `bindPager()`. Their pages say what each takes and returns.
