# Motion

Motion in the kit says one thing: this object went there. A selection slides, a card grows into its detail, a list
makes room. Everything else changes color and stays put. The rules are in the [design protocol](/protocol), section 8;
the patterns below are the movements themselves, ready to add like any component.

<MotionCurves />

## The budget

- **What may move:** selection indicators, switch thumbs, surfaces arriving, regions opening, lists reflowing,
  one element becoming another, an element under the finger, and one screen replacing another. Buttons never
  move; numbers and text never animate.
- **How long:** nothing over 600ms, and one moving thing per region.
- **Exits** use the ease curve and never overshoot.
- **Reduced motion** sets every duration to 0. Patterns built on the tokens need nothing else.

## Patterns

Eight patterns, grouped by the question they answer.

### Arriving and leaving

Something that was not there now is, or the other way round: a panel, a group of rows, a region that opens.

<Catalog kind="motion" group="arriving" />

### Moving

Something that is already there changes place: a selection, the items of a list, an element under the finger.

<Catalog kind="motion" group="moving" />

### Becoming

One thing turns into another, so the person never loses track of what they opened.

<Catalog kind="motion" group="becoming" />

### Navigating

The whole view changes to another one, and the direction says where it sits in the flow.
