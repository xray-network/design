# Icons

The kit's icons, all in one outline style: **interface icons**, **social** networks and messengers, and the **crypto**
chains XRAY works with. All are 24×24 and take the text color. Click an icon to copy its name.

To put an icon into your project, click it to copy its name, then ask your coding agent:

<CodeBlock wrapCode lang="text"><pre className="shiki css-variables" data-lang="text" tabIndex={0}><code><span className="line"><span>Add the XRAY/DESIGN icon "bell" to the notifications button.</span></span></code></pre></CodeBlock>

The agent copies the Icon component into your UI folder if it is not there yet, and uses the icon in the form your
project needs.
