# Icons

The kit's icons: outline **interface icons** and filled **brand marks** for social networks and chains. All are
24×24 and take the text color. Click an icon to copy its name.

Use one in React with `import { bell } from "./icons.js"` and `<Icon icon={bell} />`, or in HTML with
`<svg data-x-icon="bell"></svg>` and `initIcons()`. Sizes, stroke and adding your own icons are described on the
[Icon](/components/icon) component page.
